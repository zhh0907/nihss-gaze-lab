Checking browser video formats…

Analysis uses unmirrored coordinates. Left/right refer to the subject. Eye position is normalized by half the eye width, not degrees.

Optional for trajectories; required for a complete exam summary. Preview the video and label actual times. No phase order is assumed.

Research settings

Camera intrinsics are estimated. Lens distortion, auto-cropping and 3D eye shape can leave residual errors.

Research limits: |yaw| ≤35°, |pitch| ≤25°, |roll| ≤35°; eye width ≥24 px. These are unvalidated defaults, not accuracy guarantees.

Synthetic landmarks test the workflow, bypassing the detector. Demo results do not establish clinical performance.
